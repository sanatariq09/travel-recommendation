About Us

Wayfarer started as a shared notebook of places we loved. Today it is a small team helping travellers choose destinations with confidence.

Our mission

Make trip planning feel like exploring, not homework, with clear recommendations and honest descriptions.

What we cover

Beaches, temples and cities across the world, each shown with photos and a short guide.

Our team

Anna (editor), Malik (developer) and Sofia (photography) keep the collection growing.

Guidelines for team members

Check every place

Before adding a destination, confirm its name, location and description are accurate and up to date.

Use original photos

Upload only photos you own or have permission to use, and give each one a clear file name.

Reply to travellers

Answer messages from the Contact Us page within two working days, politely and clearly.
